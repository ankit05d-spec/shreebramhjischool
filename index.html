About Us

Shree Gautam Muni Tiwari

Founder & Visionary Leader

Shree Gautam Muni Tiwari is a senior urban journalist with an extensive background in media and education. His passion for transforming education and empowering students from underprivileged backgrounds led to the establishment of Shree Brahma Ji Adarsh HP School.

With a deep commitment to social responsibility, he believes that quality education should be accessible to all, regardless of economic status. His visionary approach has made the school a beacon of hope for hundreds of students in the community.

As a farmer and journalist, Shree Gautam Muni Tiwari combines practical wisdom with progressive thinking, creating an educational institution that values both tradition and innovation.

📖 School History

Shree Brahma Ji Adarsh HP School was established with the aim of providing accessible, quality education to underprivileged students. Since its inception in 2008-09, the school has been serving the community with dedication and commitment to educational excellence.

To be a leading educational institution that transforms lives through quality education, cultural values, and holistic development, creating responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.

📋 Admission Process & Requirements

  1. Fill the online admission form completely
  2. Submit recent passport-size photograph (4x6 cm)
  3. Provide birth certificate or Aadhar proof
  4. Parents meet with Principal for interview
  5. School may conduct basic assessment
  6. Confirmation of admission with fee payment
  7. Student ID card will be generated from submitted data
