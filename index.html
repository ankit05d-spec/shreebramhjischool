About Us

📖 School History

Shree Brahma Ji Adarsh HP School was established with the aim of providing accessible, quality education to underprivileged students. Since its inception in 2008-09, the school has been serving the community with dedication.

👨‍💼 Founder

Shree Gautam Muni Tiwari - A senior urban journalist with extensive background in media and education. He is a visionary leader committed to transforming education and empowering students from all backgrounds.

🎯 Mission & Vision

Mission To provide quality education at affordable costs to all students, ensuring their holistic development
Vision To create a generation of responsible, educated citizens who contribute positively to society

📋 Admission Process & Requirements

  1. Fill the online admission form completely
  2. Submit recent passport-size photograph (4x6 cm)
  3. Provide birth certificate or Aadhar proof
  4. Parents meet with Principal for interview
  5. School may conduct basic assessment
  6. Confirmation of admission with fee payment
  7. Student ID card will be generated from submitted data

⚙️ System for Student Identity Card Generation

  • ✅ Valid student photograph (JPG/PNG format)
  • ✅ Aadhar number (for digital identity)
  • ✅ Complete personal information
  • ✅ Admission form completion
  • ✅ ID cards printed and laminated within 7 days
